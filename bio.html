sean thomas

sean brings 24 years of experience to his craft, including a decade spent in top salons across los angeles and orange county. as a high-level stylist, colorist, and session hairdresser, he’s worked behind the scenes in music, fashion, tv, and commercial work—creating looks for armani privé, ketel one, the pussycat dolls, and american idol contestants. he also appeared on the oxygen network’s tease, winning the silver scissors award.

a lifelong learner, sean has studied advanced techniques with vidal sassoon, toni & guy, bumble and bumble, paul mitchell, and more. his curiosity and diverse education shape an approach rooted in art, architecture, and thoughtful engineering. he believes beauty and structure go hand in hand, and that great hair should feel effortless, intentional, and tailored to real life.

whether he’s behind the chair or behind a torch for his custom metal fabrication studio, de stijl forms, sean thrives in creative spaces; designing elegant, purposeful solutions that fit each client’s lifestyle and vision.
